Features

About Supply Tech

Everything your healthcare team needs to manage supplies efficiently — from the supply room to the operating table.

Easy, At Your Fingertips
Supplies are searchable and recognizable in seconds, with clear images, standardized names, and intuitive filters so staff spend less time hunting and more time caring for patients. One interface shows exactly what's available, where it is, and how to get it — reducing phone calls, binder checks, and workarounds.
Accurate, Real Time
The system reduces waste by surfacing preferred items, flagging substitutions, and highlighting expiring or low-use products before they become landfill. Data on ordering patterns and utilization helps hospitals right-size stock levels, shrinking their environmental footprint while maintaining clinical readiness.
Simple, Sustainable
Live inventory status and back-order alerts mean clinicians see the true availability of every item before placing an order, avoiding last-minute scrambles. Real-time dashboards give supply chain teams actionable signals on demand spikes, substitution impacts, and stock risks — enabling proactive, not reactive, management.
Barcode & Camera Scan-Out
Operators scan items out using a barcode scanner or tablet camera. Every transaction is recorded instantly with quantity, time, and unit location — giving managers a complete picture of what was used and when.
Automated Reorder Alerts
When stock drops below the minimum threshold, managers receive an automatic alert. No more manual checks or surprise shortages — the system proactively protects your supply levels 24/7.
Tablet-Ready Interface
Designed for tablets mounted in each unit. Operators log in, scan items, and confirm quantities in seconds. No complex training required — if you can use a smartphone, you can use ORCA.
The Problem We Solve

We're here to solve the big problems

Chronic inefficiencies in healthcare supply chains drive major financial, operational, and environmental costs in Canada and worldwide.

  • 01
    Hospital waste is extensive: Canadians generate an average of 706 kg of hospital waste per person, with biomedical and supply-related materials among the top contributors.
  • 02
    Resource shortages persist: Over 270 medication shortages were reported in 2025 alone, exposing gaps in supply chain resilience and patient safety.
  • 03
    Outdated tracking systems: Including binder-based and fragmented inventory management — continue to inflate costs and reduce efficiency.
  • 04
    Environmental toll: Canada's health system contributes 4.6% of national greenhouse gas emissions, largely from single-use medical supplies.
