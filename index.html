Features

Everything your team needs

From the supply room to the operating table — complete visibility across every unit, floor, and site.

Barcode & Camera Scan-Out
Operators scan items out using a barcode scanner or tablet camera. Every transaction is recorded instantly with quantity, time, and location.
Real-Time Analytics
Managers see daily usage, high-demand items, and stock levels across every unit and site — updated live as items are scanned out.
Automated Reorder Alerts
When stock drops below the minimum threshold, managers receive an automatic alert by email. No more manual checks or surprise shortages.
General Storage Mapping
Items in central storage are assigned shelf locations A–F with sections 1–4. Staff find any item in seconds with the visual shelf map.
Tablet-Ready Interface
Designed for tablets mounted in each unit. Operators log in, scan items, and confirm quantities in seconds — no training required.
Multi-Site Management
Manage multiple buildings, floors, and units from a single dashboard. Each organization's data is completely private and isolated.
How it works

Up and running in one day

No complex IT setup. No hardware to buy. Just sign up, configure your units, and start tracking.

1
Sign up and create your organization
Register your hospital or clinic. Add your sites, floors, and units. The whole setup takes under 30 minutes.
2
Build your item master
Add every supply item with photos, barcodes, and min/max quantities. Import existing lists or add items one by one.
3
Mount tablets in each unit
Open the tablet interface on any tablet or browser. Assign it to a unit. Operators can start scanning immediately.
4
Track, alert, and reorder automatically
As items are scanned out, stock levels update in real time. Alerts fire automatically when items hit the minimum threshold.
